# FoodSave 2.0 Research Proposal

## Problem

Food donations can expire before pickup because of limited shelf-life, delayed responses and operational constraints.

## Proposed contribution

A reproducible machine-learning pipeline that estimates expiry risk and exposes the result as transparent decision support for authorized FoodSave users.

## Research stages

1. Define the target and feature set.
2. Collect and document operational data.
3. Clean and engineer features.
4. Establish a rule-based baseline.
5. Train Logistic Regression and Random Forest.
6. Evaluate with temporal holdout data when possible.
7. Analyze false positives and false negatives.
8. Perform feature-group ablation.
9. Integrate predictions into NGO/admin workflows.
10. Evaluate operational outcomes separately from model metrics.

## Expected evidence

Reproducible code, dataset provenance, model comparison, error analysis, experiment reports, working dashboard and a technical report.
